Kubuntu is free to download and use. If you would like to buy something to go with it, such as a laptop built for Kubuntu, a desktop assistant or a hat, these are the places to start.

Prefer to give something directly? You can donate to Kubuntu or volunteer your time.

Kubuntu Focus

Kubuntu Focus sells laptops and a mini PC that ship with Kubuntu 26.04 LTS and the Kubuntu Focus tools. The range currently includes the Zr, M2, Ar, Xe (14″ and 16″) and NX models.

Order a Kubuntu Focus Visit kfocus.org

Kubuntu Focus merchandise

Kubuntu Focus runs a shop with hats, hoodies and mugs. It is run by Kubuntu Focus, not by the Kubuntu project.

Browse the Focus shop

Klara

Klara is an AI assistant for the Plasma desktop, built first for Kubuntu. It is sold by Timmisun Limited and is not part of Kubuntu itself. Timmisun Limited contributes 25% of every Klara sale to the Kubuntu project.

Visit meetklara.ai
