Kubuntu is free to download and use. If you would like to buy something to go with it, such as a laptop built for Kubuntu or a desktop assistant, here are some places to start.

Kubuntu Focus and Klara are independent businesses. Listing them here is not a Kubuntu endorsement or guarantee, and the Kubuntu project does not sell or support them.

Prefer to give something directly? You can donate to Kubuntu or volunteer your time.

Kubuntu Focus

Kubuntu Focus sells laptops and a mini PC that ship with Kubuntu LTS and the Kubuntu Focus tools. See kfocus.org for the current range.

Order a Kubuntu Focus

Kubuntu Focus merchandise

The Kubuntu Focus shop sells bags and hoodies. It is run by Kubuntu Focus, not by the Kubuntu project.

Browse the Focus shop

Klara

Klara is an optional AI assistant for the Plasma desktop, built first for Kubuntu. It opens apps, installs software and teaches you the desktop. It is sold by Timmisun Limited and is not part of Kubuntu itself. Timmisun Limited contributes 25% of every Klara sale to the Kubuntu project.

Visit meetklara.ai
