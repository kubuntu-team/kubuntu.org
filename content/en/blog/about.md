# About the Community Blog

The Kubuntu Community Blog is a space for **community members** to publish tips, tutorials, opinions, and project stories.

## Important

- Posts reflect the **views of their authors**, not necessarily the Kubuntu Council or the Kubuntu project as a whole.
- Official announcements and Council communications continue to appear in [News](/news/).
- Discussion belongs on [Kubuntu Discourse](https://discourse.ubuntu.com/c/flavors/kubuntu/187) (and other community channels). The blog is for longer-form writing; Discourse is for conversation.

## Want to write?

Follow the step-by-step guide: **[Write a Community Blog post](/blog/write/)**.

Also see [Contribute](/community/contribute/). Every post needs an `author` display name and a stable `author_slug`.
