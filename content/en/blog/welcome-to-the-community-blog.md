Welcome to the **Kubuntu Community Blog**.

This section is separate from [News](/news/). News stays the home for official announcements and release notes. The blog is for **community voice**: tips, walkthroughs, opinions, and stories from people who use and contribute to Kubuntu every day.

### What belongs here

- How-to guides and Plasma tips
- Contributor diaries and packaging notes
- Accessibility, artwork, and localization stories
- Friendly opinion pieces clearly marked as personal views

### What stays elsewhere

- Official Council statements and release announcements → [News](/news/)
- Questions and discussion → [Kubuntu Discourse](https://discourse.ubuntu.com/c/flavors/kubuntu/187)

### Want to publish?

Fork the [kubuntu.org](https://github.com/kubuntu-team/kubuntu.org) repository, add a post under `content/en/blog/` using the blog archetype, and open a pull request. Every post needs:

- `author` — display name
- `author_slug` — stable id (lowercase, hyphenated)
- optional `author_url` — homepage or profile link

Happy writing — and see you in the [Hall of Fame](/blog/hall-of-fame/)!
